About me

My name is Fildzah Zata, a curious UI/UX designer with more than two years of experience in crafting user-friendly HRIS software solutions, complemented by over a year of background in industrial product design and craftsmanship.

My journey into the world of UX design was sparked during my time in design school when I discovered Joseph Joseph's kitchenware products. I was amazed by their way of thinking in solving problems in the kitchen through intuitive physical design. Since then, I've been passionate about improving lives through thoughtful, human-centered design.

Learn to design, Design to learn

"Fail hard, fail often." I believe that understanding design is a process that takes time to work through. Everyone has a unique path and different perspectives. Feedback is an opportunity to grow for me, both as a designer and as a person.
